Why IIT1729?

When a friend visited the mathematician Srinivasa Ramanujan in hospital and called his taxi number, 1729, a dull number, Ramanujan replied that it was very interesting. He had noticed what others missed.

That habit of noticing is what JEE rewards. Here you do not memorise formulas. You learn why they work, so a new problem looks like a familiar one.

How we teach

Start with life

Slope is the steepness of a road. Probability is a weather forecast. You meet the idea before the formula.

Build up slowly

Basic level first, then board level, then JEE Main, then JEE Advanced, within the same chapter.

Practise with feedback

Problem sets and tests for every chapter, with mistakes explained, not just marked.
